About the Movie

Avengers: Endgame is one of my favourite movies. It is a superhero movie based on Marvel characters. The Avengers come together to defeat Thanos and save the universe. The movie is full of action, emotions, suspense, teamwork and sacrifice.

My Favourite Character

My favourite character is Iron Man. He is intelligent, brave and hardworking. His sacrifice makes him one of my favourite characters.
